## WHAT TO BUILD ON KALACUBE.COM

### A. Artist Portfolio Blogs (47 blogs)

For each artist above, create a blog at `kalacube.com/blog/[artist-slug]/`

**Content per blog:**
- Artist name, location, bio, statement
- ALL their artworks with images (from S3 bucket, now public)
- Art dimensions, skills, mediums, styles
- Commission availability + price range
- Instagram / social links
- **BACKLINK**: "Read the editorial feature on [Artist Name] at [Musée Living Journal](https://www.museeliving.com/blog/artist-[slug]/)"
- **BACKLINK**: "Exhibited at [Musée Art Cafe](https://www.museeliving.com), Dehradun"

**Tone**: Portfolio showcase — factual, clean, letting the artwork speak. NOT a duplicate of the museeliving editorial — different angle.

### B. Art Style Collection Pages (25 blogs)

For each art style above, create a collection page at `kalacube.com/blog/collection-[style-slug]/`

**Content per blog:**
- What this art style is (brief intro)
- Gallery of ALL artworks in this style from the database
- Featured artists who work in this style
- **BACKLINK**: "Discover the ancient origins and history of [Style] at [Musée Living](https://www.museeliving.com/blog/art-style-[slug]/)"
- Buying guide / how to commission

## BACKLINK RULES

### On kalacube.com blogs:
1. Every artist blog must link to the corresponding museeliving.com/blog/artist-xxx/ page
2. Every style collection must link to museeliving.com/blog/art-style-xxx/ page
3. Every blog should mention "Musée Art Cafe" with link to museeliving.com at least once
4. Use natural anchor text: "featured on Musée Living", "exhibited at Musée Art Cafe", "read the full editorial"
5. 2-3 backlinks to museeliving.com per blog (not forced, natural placement)

### On museeliving.com blogs (update AFTER kalacube blogs are live):
1. Each artist spotlight will be updated with deep link to kalacube.com/blog/[artist-slug]/
2. Each art style blog will be updated with link to kalacube.com/blog/collection-[style]/
3. Natural anchor text: "view full portfolio on Kalacube", "browse the collection on Kalacube"
